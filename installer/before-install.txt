Voice Assistant works fully offline once installed. Before you continue, note:

WHAT THIS INSTALLER DOES
  - Installs the app.
  - Downloads the speech and voice models it needs (about 2.4 GB in total:
    Vosk wake-word model, Whisper speech recognition, Piper voice).
    This needs an internet connection and about 5 GB of free disk space
    during setup.
  - Optionally downloads and installs Tesseract OCR (used by "click on ...").

WHAT YOU INSTALL YOURSELF
  - Ollama (https://ollama.com/download) - runs the AI conversation.
    After installing it, run:   ollama pull qwen2.5:7b
    Ollama must be running whenever you ask the assistant a general question.
  - Npcap (https://npcap.com/#download) - only for "scan the network".
    Choose "Install Npcap in WinPcap API-compatible Mode".
    Network scans also need the assistant to be started as administrator.
